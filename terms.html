The service consists of delivering a single targeted technical diagnostic audit for one chosen domain of the client's e-commerce operations. The client selects one focus area during ordering:

  1. Google Ads Budget Bleed & Search Term Audit: Identification of wasted spend on irrelevant search terms, broad match cannibalization review, and structured negative keyword export.
  2. Merchant Center & Product Feed Integrity: In-depth check of disapproved catalog items, missing GTIN/EAN identifiers, and title attribute optimization.
  3. GA4 & E-Commerce Tracking Diagnostics: Audit of purchase event precision, Consent Mode v2 attribution losses, and data discrepancies against store backend.
  4. Checkout Friction & Funnel Leak Scan: End-to-end checkout flow review, mobile display barriers, and checkout drop-off reduction checklist.

Deliverable: The client receives an electronic PDF report (approximately 3 pages) containing verified technical findings, financial impact ranking, and concrete tasks ready for internal teams or technical vendors.
