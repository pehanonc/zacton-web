1. Primary Budget Leaks Identified

Leak A: Uncontrolled Broad Match Cannibalization

Broad match keywords matched high-volume informational queries (e.g., "how to repair...", "manual pdf", "free alternatives"). These terms yielded zero conversions while spending 248 € over 312 wasted clicks.

Recommended Action: Restrict queries to Phrase match; attach the provided Negative Global List.

Leak B: Brand Name Cannibalization in Performance Max

Performance Max campaigns spent 38% of total budget bidding on the store's own brand terms. This inflated ROAS to an artificial 1,150% while stealing traffic that would convert organically or via low-cost Brand Search.

Recommended Action: Apply a Brand Exclusion list directly in PMax settings.

Leak C: Missing Shared Negative Keyword Library

New search ad groups launched without inheriting baseline exclusions. Generic terms such as "login", "forum", and competitor support numbers were actively consuming spend.

Recommended Action: Deploy Account-Level Shared Negative Library immediately.
