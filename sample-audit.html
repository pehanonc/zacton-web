3. In-Depth Technical Breakdown of Identified Leaks

Leak A: Unconstrained Broad Match Category Bleed Monthly Loss: 246.80 €
Generic category keywords set to Broad Match without stringent conversion volume signals are capturing broad non-transactional traffic. Ads are repeatedly served to users researching user manuals in PDF format, general consumer reviews, or medical side-effects. These queries generate high click volumes with 0% checkout initiation.
Required Fix: Restructure generic category ad groups into Phrase Match and deploy the provided Master Negative List targeting informational modifiers (manual, pdf, reviews, forum, dosage).
Leak B: Performance Max Brand Cannibalization ROAS Distortion: ~38%
The primary Performance Max asset group operates without a Brand Exclusion List. The algorithm is aggressively bidding on the store's exact brand name, capturing returning visitors who would otherwise convert via free organic search. This artificially inflates reported campaign ROAS while yielding negligible incremental revenue.
Required Fix: Create a Brand List within Account Settings and attach it as a strict negative exclusion to all generic Performance Max campaigns.
Leak C: Missing Shared Account-Level Negative Library Monthly Loss: 162.40 €
The account lacks shared negative keyword lists applied across newly created campaigns. Each new product group repeatedly pays to discover predictable negative terms (competitor names of brands not stocked, customer portal logins, second-hand portal queries, or warranty return requests).
Required Fix: Import the centralized master negative list directly to Account-Level Negative Keywords.
