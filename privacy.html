Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how personal and commercial business data is handled by Zacton, operated by Peter Haratík, IČO: 40148032, contact email: contact@zacton.com.

1. Scope of Service

Zacton provides professional B2B technical diagnostics and performance audits for e-commerce operators, including Google Ads search term reviews, Google Merchant Center integrity checks, GA4 tracking verification, and checkout UX friction analysis.

2. Data Collection & Intake Information

When ordering an audit, we process only the minimum operational information necessary to execute the service:

  • Contact & Billing Details: Name, business email, company billing data, and transaction IDs (processed securely via Stripe; payment card details are never stored on our servers).
  • Audit Parameters: Store URL, XML product feed links, Google Ads 10-digit Customer ID (CID), and Google Analytics account identifiers.
  • System Logs: IP address, device type, and referral data captured via Cloudflare security and caching layers.

3. Access to Client Platforms (Read-Only Policy)

We operate under a strict read-only / viewer access standard. Clients are requested to invite Zacton solely with reporting or viewer permissions to Google Ads, Google Analytics 4, or Google Merchant Center accounts. We do not request, nor accept, administrative or operational write access to modify live campaigns, bids, budgets, or shop backends.

4. Data Retention and Erasure

  • Account audit access should be revoked by the client immediately upon report delivery. Any remaining shared permissions are removed from our side within 14 days of order completion.
  • Downloaded reporting extracts, temporary CSV exports, and diagnostic notes are permanently purged within 30 days of report delivery.
  • Invoices and transaction metadata are retained exclusively to meet statutory Slovak accounting and tax retention periods.

5. Third-Party Processors

We work exclusively with compliant infrastructure providers for web delivery, billing, and reporting:

  • Stripe, Inc.: Payment gateway and checkout processing.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: DNS routing, SSL encryption, and CDN caching.
  • MailerSend: Transactional email and intake routing.

6. Client Rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy regulations, clients have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete erasure of any stored contact data. Inquiries should be directed to contact@zacton.com.
